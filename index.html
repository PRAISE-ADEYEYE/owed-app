Credit sales run on memory and paper.

Most traders sell on credit because customers expect it. Then the notebook gets wet, a page goes missing, or a customer disputes the amount.

Money is lost not because customers refuse to pay, but because nobody can say exactly what is owed.

One clear record per customer.

Owed keeps a running balance for everyone who buys from you, and saves it on a server so it is still there tomorrow.

  1. Write the creditEnter the name, amount and what they took. It takes a few seconds.
  2. Record each paymentWhen they pay part or all, add it. The balance updates straight away.
  3. See your totalThe number at the top of your book is what you are owed across everyone.
